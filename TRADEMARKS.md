# Trademark policy

The code of BitAgentsHub is free under the [MIT License](LICENSE). The
name "BitAgentsHub" (BAH) and its logos are not covered by that license: they
identify the official project by Josue Bladimir Morales Llanganate
(MrT-coder).

## What you may do without asking

- Say that your project, theme or article works with, is built for, or is
  based on BitAgentsHub.
- Use the name to refer to the official project, in reviews, tutorials,
  talks and package listings.
- Publish themes for BitAgentsHub under your own name.

## Forks and modified versions

A fork is welcome. Give it a different name and make clear it is not the
official BitAgentsHub, for example "myoffice, a fork of BitAgentsHub".
Keep the original `LICENSE` file, as the MIT License requires.

## Themes and other people's trademarks

A theme may be inspired by a movie, show, game or brand. It must not include
their logos, official artwork, or trademarked names presented as the
theme's own, and it must not suggest it is endorsed by their owners.
Themes that do may be removed from any official theme listing.

## Asking for permission

For any other use of the name or logos, open an issue or contact the
maintainer through GitHub.
